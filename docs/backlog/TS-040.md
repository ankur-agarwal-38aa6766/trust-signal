## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Obtain operator-approved jurisdiction/source/purpose policies for raw documents, extracts, search indexes, case snapshots, audit and logs; record license renewal/revocation.
- [ ] Implement restricted correction/access/deletion workflows and lawful hold handling with identity verification and minimal non-sensitive audit references.
- [ ] Propagate permitted purge/redaction across caches, indexes, exports and backup lifecycle; document erasure limits and recompute/invalidate affected assessments.
- [ ] Test retention enforcement and restore behavior; do not infer legal requirements from a generic default or alter live retention without approval.

## Verification
