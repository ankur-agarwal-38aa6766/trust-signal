- [ ] Obtain operator-approved jurisdiction/source/purpose policies for raw documents, extracts, search indexes, case snapshots, audit and logs; record license renewal/revocation.

- [ ] Propagate permitted purge/redaction across caches, indexes, exports and backup lifecycle; document erasure limits and recompute/invalidate affected assessments.
