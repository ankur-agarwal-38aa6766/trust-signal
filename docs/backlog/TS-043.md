## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Choose source-specific freshness/latency/availability targets, alert ownership and incident response procedures; distinguish provider delay from system delay.
- [ ] Enforce per-case/source/tenant quotas, admission control and fair bounded concurrency for ingestion, inference and monitoring; account cost monitors are not complete spend caps.
- [ ] Implement authorized cancel/resume semantics that stop new work, preserve persisted evidence and reconcile in-flight writes without duplicate case owners.
- [ ] Verify overload, queue saturation, budget exhaustion and cancellation during fetch/write/aggregation; connect approved alerts through TS-034 and notifications through TS-028.
